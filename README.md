# Cyber Security Series Notes: Macquarie University on Coursera

Simple, plain-English study notes for a **three-part Cyber Security series** taught by **Macquarie University** on **Coursera**. The notes are written from scratch to teach each topic, with every term explained, real examples, and short summaries.

**Live website:** https://taha-cybersecurity-notes.netlify.app/

**Author:** [Taha Anwar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/taha-anwar-1977231ba/)

## The three courses

| Part | Course | Modules in the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cyber Security: Data Privacy | 9 topic groups |
| 2 | Cyber Security: Data Security and Information Privacy | 6 modules |
| 3 | Cyber Security: Security of AI | 6 modules + glossary |

The site has three tabs (one per course). Each course tab has its own module tabs. The tab you are on is highlighted, and scrolling stays inside the module you are reading.

## What is covered

### Part 1: Data Privacy
1. **Privacy foundations:** what data privacy is, personal information, direct and indirect identifiers, data minimisation, purpose limitation, data retention, security controls.
2. **Security and cryptography:** encryption (at rest and in transit), data masking, pseudonymisation.
3. **De-identification:** omission, generalisation, aggregation, rounding, adding noise, differential privacy (simple), why it is never 100% safe.
4. **Re-identification:** internal and external risks, vulnerable datasets and groups, linkage attacks, data situation audits, dynamic risk analysis.
5. **Privacy by Design and AI governance:** privacy impact assessments (PIA), Privacy by Design, algorithmic impact assessments (AIA), algorithmic bias, managing AI risk.
6. **Data sharing:** open data, sharing checklists, ethical obligations.
7. **Incident response:** privacy incidents, the 8-step response lifecycle, response plans and teams, simulations, the Cyber Kill Chain.
8. **Breach notification:** Australia's Notifiable Data Breach (NDB) scheme and its 3-part test, writing a clear notice, media and stakeholder communication, insider trading, lessons learned.
9. **Cheat sheets:** 20 key differences and the top 14 golden rules.

### Part 2: Data Security and Information Privacy
1. **Threats, risks and vulnerabilities:** data security vs privacy, the CIA triad, hashing, IS network vulnerabilities (SQL injection, WannaCry, IoT, Mirai), social engineering, the four re-identification attacks, entropy and uniqueness.
2. **Quantifying and evaluating privacy risk:** uniqueness, uniformity, linkable tokens, correlation, threat models, the privacy spectrum, Shannon entropy with worked examples.
3. **De-identification methods for data release:** naive techniques, k-anonymity, l-diversity, t-closeness, the attacks on each, privacy vs utility.
4. **Probable privacy to provable privacy:** differential privacy, epsilon, global vs local DP, Laplace, Exponential and Randomised Response mechanisms, sensitivity.
5. **Privacy-preserving record linkage:** blocking, Bloom filters, q-grams, matching methods, precision, recall and F1.
6. **Privacy and security tools and frameworks:** ARX, GPG, symmetric vs asymmetric encryption, VeraCrypt, NIST Privacy Framework, Essential Eight, Five Safes, post-quantum cryptography.

### Part 3: Security of AI
1. **AI systems and security basics:** machine learning types, neural networks, transformers, GANs, normal cyber threats, deepfakes, adversarial attacks, poisoning, backdoors, model theft.
2. **AI in industrial systems (ICS/OT):** anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, new AI risks, Stuxnet, T-Mobile 2023, DevSecOps, MITRE ATLAS.
3. **Security and ethics risks inside AI models:** threat models, poisoning and backdoors, membership inference, model inversion, model stealing, alignment, privacy and ethics.
4. **Defences (AI controls) and testing:** adversarial training, certified robustness, differential privacy, watermarking, content filters, metaprompts, RAG, red/blue/purple teams, benchmarks.
5. **Responsible AI, regulation and governance:** fairness and bias, EU AI Act, US AI Bill of Rights, ISO/IEC 42001, governance frameworks, best practices.
6. **Future of AI and emerging risks:** future applications, history of AI, narrow/general/super AI, new technologies and their risks, best practices.
7. **Glossary:** about 70 terms in plain words.
